About us

DABA Enterprise™ · AI Shopping Copilot

What we build

AI Shopping Copilot is a browser extension that reads the product page you're already looking at and tells you whether the thing is worth buying — a 0–100 buying score, a check on whether the reviews look genuine, and up to twelve months of price history so you can see whether the discount is real. It works across roughly twenty-five retailers including Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Best Buy, Target, Etsy, AliExpress, Alibaba, Shein and Temu.

The reasoning behind the score is published in full on our methodology page — the weightings, what each band means, and where the model is unreliable.

Why it exists

Online product pages are designed to close a sale, not to inform a decision. The star average is inflated, the "was" price in the strikethrough often never existed, and the top reviews are frequently the least representative ones. Working out the truth means opening five tabs and cross-checking — which nobody does at 11pm for a $40 purchase.

We built the thing that does that cross-check in the four seconds you were going to spend hesitating anyway. The goal is a tool that will tell you not to buy something, because a tool that only ever says yes is an advertisement.

How we make money

Two ways, and we'd rather you knew both up front.

  • Pro subscriptions. A$7 per month or A$49 per year removes the five-analyses-a-day limit and unlocks the score breakdown, review-trust detail and price history. This is the revenue we actually want.
  • Affiliate commissions. Some product and "Buy" links shown by the extension are affiliate links — including through the Amazon Associates Program, AliExpress and Temu. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission. It never costs you more.

The scoring model has no access to which merchants pay us a commission, so it cannot favour them. If that ever stopped being true we would say so here first.

What we don't do

  • We don't track your browsing. Nothing runs until you open the extension and ask for an analysis.
  • We don't store the product pages we analyse, and we don't tie them to any identity.
  • We don't sell data. There is no data broker relationship, now or planned.
  • We don't require an account, an email address or an API key to use the free plan.
